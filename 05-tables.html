Working with Tables

Basic Table Structure

Tables are made up of rows (tr) and cells (td for data, th for headers):

Name Age City
John 25 New York
Sarah 30 London
Mike 28 Tokyo

Table with Header, Body, and Footer

For better structure, use thead, tbody, and tfoot:

Product Price Quantity Total
Laptop $999 1 $999
Mouse $25 2 $50
Keyboard $75 1 $75
Grand Total $1,124

Colspan and Rowspan

Use colspan to merge cells horizontally, rowspan to merge vertically:

Full Name Email
John Doe john@example.com
Jane Smith jane@example.com

Name Contact
Email Phone
John Doe john@example.com 123-456-7890
Jane Smith jane@example.com 098-765-4321

Table with Caption

Use caption to add a title to your table:

Monthly Sales Report - 2024
Month Sales Profit

Table Attributes & Best Practices

Accessibility

  • Always use <th> for header cells
  • Use <caption> to describe the table
  • Use scope attribute on th elements (scope="col" or scope="row")
  • Keep tables simple - avoid complex nested structures when possible

Example with scope attribute

Name Score Grade
Alice 95 A
Bob 87 B

Practice Exercise

Create a table with:

  1. A caption
  2. Header row with at least 4 columns
  3. At least 5 data rows
  4. Use thead, tbody, and tfoot
  5. One example of colspan or rowspan
  6. Proper styling (borders, padding, etc.)

Understanding Headings and Paragraphs

Main Title (h1)

There should typically be only ONE h1 per page - it represents the main topic.

Section Heading (h2)

Use h2 for major sections of your page. This is a paragraph explaining the section.

Subsection (h3)

Use h3 for subsections within h2 sections.

Further Subsection (h4)

You can continue with h4, h5, and h6 as needed, though most pages don't go deeper than h3 or h4.

How to Make Coffee

Introduction

Coffee is one of the most popular beverages in the world. This guide will teach you how to make a perfect cup.

Ingredients

You'll need coffee beans, water, and optionally milk or sugar.

Instructions

Step 1: Prepare Your Equipment

Gather your coffee maker, filters, and measuring tools.

Step 2: Measure the Coffee

Use about 2 tablespoons of coffee grounds per 6 ounces of water.

Step 3: Brew

Follow your coffee maker's instructions to brew the coffee.

Tips

Use fresh, quality beans for the best flavor. Store coffee in an airtight container.

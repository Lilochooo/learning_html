Working with Lists

Unordered Lists (ul)

Unordered lists are used when the order of items doesn't matter:

  • Apples
  • Bananas
  • Oranges
  • Grapes

Different Bullet Styles

  • Disc bullets (default)
  • Circle bullets
  • Square bullets
  • No bullets

Ordered Lists (ol)

Ordered lists are used when the order of items matters (like steps in a recipe):

  1. First step
  2. Second step
  3. Third step
  4. Fourth step

Different Numbering Styles

  1. Numbers (1, 2, 3...)
  1. Uppercase letters (A, B, C...)
  1. Lowercase letters (a, b, c...)
  1. Roman numerals uppercase (I, II, III...)
  1. Roman numerals lowercase (i, ii, iii...)

Starting from a Specific Number

  1. This list starts at 5
  2. Then 6
  3. Then 7

How to Make Scrambled Eggs

  1. Crack 2 eggs into a bowl
  2. Add a pinch of salt and pepper
  3. Whisk the eggs until well mixed
  4. Heat a pan with butter over medium heat
  5. Pour in the eggs
  6. Stir gently until cooked
  7. Serve hot
